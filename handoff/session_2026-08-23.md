## Decisions Made
- **All three "fan" sections (Hero, Booking Site, Notifications) now share one visual pattern**: full-width centered text block (`containerWide` + `stack`), with a rotated card/phone fan below it, each with its own dedicated `<Section>.module.css`. Booking Site and Notifications were both restructured from their original two-column split layouts to match Hero's established look, per explicit user approval each time.
- **CSS custom properties over prop-drilling or new component variants** for per-instance sizing overrides on shared components (`PhoneFrame`'s `--phone-width`, `--island-width/height/top`): keeps the default/standalone look byte-for-byte identical while letting a specific fan context override just what it needs.
- **Inline `style` used for one-off spacing/font-size overrides** (e.g. Booking Site's and Notifications' `.grow` margin-top) instead of fighting CSS-module cascade/specificity against the shared `sections.module.css` rules — simpler and unambiguous for single-instance tweaks.
- **Cropping admin screenshots to remove the sidebar** rather than shrinking further or leaving as-is: the dense small sidebar nav text was the actual legibility problem at small rotated sizes, not overall resolution — this was verified by direct pixel inspection before cropping.
- **Telegram bot over email for the contact form**, per explicit user choice after a short recommendation (simplicity, no SMTP setup, consistent with the product's own "everything through the bot" positioning). Token/chat ID stored in `.env.local` (already covered by `.gitignore`), never hardcoded into committed source.
- **ordiset.com confirmed as the production domain** this session (via direct question, since nothing in the repo referenced any domain) — now baked into `metadataBase`, OpenGraph URLs, and `robots.ts`.
